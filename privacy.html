This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, and protected when you interact with the Services operated by Joe Murrell ("Operator," "we," "our," or "us"), including integrations that function through Discord or related platforms.

By using the Services, you agree to the collection and use of information as described in this Policy.

🧱 1. Information We Collect

We collect only the data necessary to operate and improve the Services.

A. Automatically Collected Data

When you interact with the Services (for example, using a Discord command), the following information may be recorded:

  • Discord user ID, username, and discriminator
  • Server ID and channel ID where a command is used
  • Command inputs or parameters (e.g., text entered in slash commands)
  • Timestamps and technical logs (for performance, debugging, and moderation)

B. User-Submitted Content

Some Services may allow uploading or submission of content (e.g., text files, PDFs, or prompts).
That content is processed automatically for functionality (for example, enabling search, analysis, or AI responses).

  • Uploaded data may be temporarily stored and indexed for performance.
  • Each Discord server's data is kept isolated from others.
  • We do not manually review or access your data unless required for debugging or abuse prevention.

C. Payment Information

If you subscribe or make a purchase through a third-party provider (e.g., Stripe, Patreon, Ko-fi):

  • Payment data is handled entirely by that provider.
  • We do not receive or store full payment card details.
  • We may store limited metadata such as your Discord ID or transaction reference to verify access.

⚙️ 2. How We Use Information

We use collected data strictly for operational purposes, including:

  • Responding to user commands and running bot functions
  • Improving reliability, performance, and user experience
  • Preventing abuse and maintaining security
  • Managing premium features, billing validation, or support requests
  • Complying with legal obligations (if any)

We do not sell or rent personal data.

🧠 3. AI & External Processing

Some Services use third-party APIs such as OpenAI, Anthropic, or similar AI providers to process text or generate responses.

When you submit a query or upload data:

  • Relevant text content may be sent securely to these APIs.
  • Data is processed solely for the purpose of generating responses to your request.
  • We do not allow these providers to use your content for training or marketing.
  • We comply with their respective data use and retention policies.

🧱 4. Data Retention & Deletion

  • Operational logs are typically retained for up to 30 days for diagnostics and abuse prevention.
  • Uploaded or indexed content may persist while active in the system but can be deleted upon request.
  • Backups may contain residual data for limited periods due to system processes.

Requesting Data Deletion

You may request deletion of your data by emailing joemurrell@gmail.com or contacting through GitHub.
Include your Discord user ID and relevant server name so we can locate and remove associated data.

🛡️ 5. Data Sharing & Disclosure

We do not share personal information with third parties except:

  • Platform providers such as Discord or OpenAI, to fulfill functionality
  • Payment processors, if you subscribe or donate
  • Law enforcement, if required by valid legal process or to prevent harm or abuse

We will never sell your personal information.

⚖️ 6. Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR)

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), processing of your data is based on:
